The project

Maritime search and rescue presents a systems problem: an autonomous aircraft must search a large area, identify a person in uncertain conditions, estimate a drifting target's location, execute a safe approach, deliver a flotation payload, and return.

My approach is to develop the autonomy and perception architecture first in simulation, establish system requirements, and progressively translate those requirements into the physical aircraft.
